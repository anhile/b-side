# B-Side 0.1.1

Fixes after the first release.

## Fixed

- **Try Again was missing on the failure screens.** When the player, the
  playlists, a playlist's tracks, a search or an Explore page could not be
  loaded, the screen said so but had no button; neither had "Sign in for
  your playlists" its Sign In. They are back.
- **A new playlist shows at once.** YouTube Music's library lists a new
  playlist a minute or two after it is made, so it was missing from Add to
  Playlist and from the Playlists page until then.
- **Dragging vibe tiles is smooth.** A tile follows the pointer as soon as
  it moves, the others make room, and it glides into its place.

## Changed

- After **Start Radio**, the strip under Vibes and Playlists says whose
  radio plays: "Radio: <track>".
- A track's menu leaves out what does not apply instead of greying it:
  Go to Artist and Go to Album without a link, Show Lyrics without lyrics.
  Now Playing's menu has no Like: the heart is next to it, and Command-L
  still works.
- When the music waits for data for a second or more, the event log gets
  a `stall` line; see
  [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/anhile/b-side/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md#the-music-cuts-out).

## Install

**This build is not signed with an Apple certificate and not notarised.**
macOS will stop it the first time, also when you had 0.1.0 before. To open
it:

1. Quit B-Side. Open `B-Side-0.1.1.dmg` and drag B-Side to Applications,
   replacing the old copy. Your account, vibes and settings stay.
2. Open B-Side. macOS says that Apple could not verify it. Click **Done**.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "B-Side was
   blocked", and click **Open Anyway**. You do this once.

Or build it yourself: with Xcode and XcodeGen, `./scripts/install.sh`
builds the same code on your Mac, and macOS does not ask about an app built
there.

The image holds one app for Apple silicon and Intel; the Intel build has
not been tried on an Intel Mac.
