# B-Side 0.1.0

The first public release. B-Side is a small, fast YouTube Music player for
the Mac: a 320 by 440 window and a record in the menu bar. It plays through
Google's own player in a hidden web view, so it uses about 175 MB of memory
where the full YouTube Music page uses about 500 MB.

## What is in it

- **Now Playing.** Artwork with a turning record, like, seek, volume and
  repeat. Live lyrics that follow the song. Up Next shows what plays after
  this track.
- **Vibes.** Tiles that start music in one click: a playlist, Liked Music
  shuffled, a track's radio, or a vibe made from your own words ("rainy
  Sunday, slow jazz, no vocals").
- **Playlists.** Your library, private playlists included. Make playlists,
  add and remove tracks.
- **Explore.** Search songs, albums, artists and playlists; artist and
  album pages.
- **Menu bar.** The current track with Previous, Play and Next, every vibe
  and playlist. Closing the window does not stop the music.
- **Media keys, AirPods and Control Center** work as with any player.
- The last track is back when you open B-Side again, paused where it
  stopped.

## Install

**This build is not signed with an Apple certificate and not notarised.**
macOS will stop it the first time. To open it:

1. Open `B-Side-0.1.0.dmg` and drag B-Side to Applications.
2. Open B-Side. macOS says that Apple could not verify it. Click **Done**.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "B-Side was
   blocked", and click **Open Anyway**. You do this once.

If you would rather not allow an unsigned app, build it yourself: with
Xcode and XcodeGen, `./scripts/install.sh` builds the same code on your Mac,
and macOS does not ask about an app built there. A signed and notarised
build will follow.

The image holds one app for Apple silicon and Intel.

## Good to know

- macOS 14 or later. B-Side is developed and tested on macOS 27 only; the
  glass surfaces and the vibes read on the Mac need macOS 26. The Intel
  build has not been tried on an Intel Mac.
- Your library needs you to sign in to Google in B-Side's own window.
  Search and radios work without an account.
- B-Side does not remove ads. Without Premium you hear them, and B-Side
  says when the music comes back.
- B-Side is not made or endorsed by Google or YouTube. It depends on
  YouTube Music's web player, and a change there can break it; see
  [Troubleshooting](https://github.com/anhile/b-side/blob/main/docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md).
- [Privacy](https://github.com/anhile/b-side/blob/main/PRIVACY.md): B-Side
  has no analytics. A vibe made from words goes to the B-Side server only
  when you turn that on in Settings.
