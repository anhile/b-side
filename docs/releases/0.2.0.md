# B-Side 0.2.0

A desktop widget, a window that drags to any size, Settings of their own,
and a calmer first launch.

## New

- **A desktop widget**, small and medium: what plays, on its artwork, with
  Play, Pause and Next. With nothing playing, it starts a vibe.
- **The window drags to any size**, from compact to large. Text and
  controls keep their size; Now Playing lays itself out around them.
- **Settings in their own window** (Command-comma, or from the menu bar),
  in three tabs: General, Playback, Diagnostics.
- **Reorder by dragging**: the tracks in Up Next and the rows of your own
  playlists. A playlist can also be renamed or deleted from its menu.
- **The source along the bottom of Now Playing**: what plays from where
  ("Vibe: Focus", "Playlist: …"), in the source's colour; a click goes
  there. It also names the output device, and Sound Output is in the menu.
- **Search starts with All**: songs, albums, artists and playlists
  together, then one kind at a time. An empty search offers a few genres.
- **A tour on the sign-in screen**: what B-Side does, one slide at a time.

## Changed

- **Signing in** happens in a B-Side panel with Google's page in full.
  Signing out works as a browser's does, so Google keeps knowing the Mac;
  it may still ask for a security code, which B-Side cannot turn off.
- **First launch on a slow or lost connection** shows one calm screen at a
  time and tries again by itself when the network is back.
- **Waiting for the music** shows as a light running along the progress
  line instead of the word "Buffering".
- **Artist and album pages** have a header in the colour of their picture.
- **Liking a track** gives the heart a small burst; its tooltip shows
  Command-L.
- The menu bar icon is the record of the Now Playing tab, and easier to
  see in dark mode.
- Rows sit on glass, with more air between them; the row that plays is
  marked in playlists and in Explore.

## Fixed

- Settings no longer crash on a laptop's screen.
- Tooltips no longer hide under the vibe tiles.
- Switching Settings tabs no longer redraws the window.

## Install

**This build is not signed with an Apple certificate and not notarised.**
macOS will stop it the first time, also when you had an earlier version.
To open it:

1. Quit B-Side. Open `B-Side.dmg` and drag B-Side to Applications,
   replacing the old copy. Your account, vibes and settings stay.
2. Open B-Side. macOS says that Apple could not verify it. Click **Done**.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "B-Side was
   blocked", and click **Open Anyway**. You do this once.

Or build it yourself: with Xcode and XcodeGen, `./scripts/install.sh`
builds the same code on your Mac, and macOS does not ask about an app built
there.

`B-Side.dmg` and `B-Side-0.2.0.dmg` are the same image; the first is what
[the link to the latest release](https://github.com/anhile/b-side/releases/latest/download/B-Side.dmg)
gets. It holds one app for Apple silicon and Intel; the Intel build has
not been tried on an Intel Mac. The widget has not been tried on a Mac
other than the one it was built on.
