# B-Side 0.3.1

Buttons that feel pressed, and artwork that stays.

## Changed

- **The controls press like keys.** Under the pointer a light capsule rises
  behind the icon; pressed, it sinks and the icon squeezes, then springs
  back. With Reduce Motion the icon only dims.
- **Artwork in lists stays put.** Covers in Playlists, Explore, Up Next and
  the strip are downloaded once and shown at once when a list comes again,
  for example after a playlist is deleted. A cover that fails to load is
  tried again instead of staying blank.

## Install

**This build is not signed with an Apple certificate and not notarised.**
macOS will stop it the first time, also when you had an earlier version.
To open it:

1. Quit B-Side. Open `B-Side.dmg` and drag B-Side to Applications,
   replacing the old copy. Your account, vibes and settings stay.
2. Open B-Side. macOS says that Apple could not verify it. Click **Done**.
3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to "B-Side was
   blocked", and click **Open Anyway**. You do this once.

Or build it yourself: with Xcode and XcodeGen, `./scripts/install.sh`
builds the same code on your Mac, and macOS does not ask about an app built
there.

`B-Side.dmg` and `B-Side-0.3.1.dmg` are the same image; the first is what
[the link to the latest release](https://github.com/anhile/b-side/releases/latest/download/B-Side.dmg)
gets. It holds one app for Apple silicon and Intel.
